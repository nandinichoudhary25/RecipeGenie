About RecipeGenie

RecipeGenie is an AI-powered smart recipe and meal assistant designed to make cooking easier, smarter, and more personalized. It combines traditional recipes with modern web technologies to create a seamless cooking experience.

Problem Statement

Many users struggle to decide what to cook daily, calculate nutrition, or find structured and reliable recipes in one place.

Our Solution

RecipeGenie provides categorized recipes, detailed preparation steps, nutritional information, and intelligent assistance powered by AI. It aims to simplify meal planning while maintaining health awareness.

Technologies Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • IBM watsonx Assistant (AI Integration)
  • IBM Cloud

Vision

The vision of RecipeGenie is to evolve into a smart cooking companion that recommends personalized meals based on user preferences, dietary needs, and available ingredients.
