Khaman (Dhokla)

A soft and fluffy steamed savory cake made from gram flour, popular as a light snack.

⏱ 30 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 150 g Serving)

Calories

180 kcal

Protein

7 g

Carbohydrates

22 g

Fat

6 g

Ingredients

For Batter:

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Eno (fruit salt)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

For Tempering:

  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 2 green chilies (slit)
  • 1 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Mix besan, turmeric, sugar, lemon juice and salt.
  2. Add water to make a smooth batter.
  3. Add Eno and mix gently.
  4. Pour batter into greased steaming tray.
  5. Steam for 15–20 minutes.
  6. Heat oil and add mustard seeds.
  7. Add curry leaves and green chilies.
  8. Pour tempering over khaman and cut into pieces.
