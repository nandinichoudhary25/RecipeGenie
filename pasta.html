Creamy Pasta

A rich and comforting creamy pasta loaded with flavor and simple ingredients.

⏱ 25 mins 🍽 Serves 2 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic (chopped)
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • Mixed vegetables (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and sauté garlic.
  3. Add cream and let it simmer.
  4. Mix in cheese, salt and pepper.
  5. Add cooked pasta and mix well.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.
