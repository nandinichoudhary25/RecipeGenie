Healthy Veg Salad

A refreshing, nutrient-rich salad packed with fresh vegetables and natural flavors.

⏱ 15 mins 🍽 Serves 2 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lettuce (chopped)
  • ½ cucumber (sliced)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • ½ carrot (grated)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash and chop all vegetables.
  2. Combine lettuce, cucumber, tomato and carrot in a bowl.
  3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Toss well and serve fresh.
