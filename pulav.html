Veg Pulav

Aromatic and flavorful Indian rice dish cooked with mixed vegetables and mild whole spices.

⏱ 35 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 250 g Serving)

Calories

300 kcal

Protein

6 g

Carbohydrates

55 g

Fat

8 g

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans)
  • 1 sliced onion
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak rice for 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pressure cooker or pan.
  3. Add cumin seeds and whole spices, sauté for a few seconds.
  4. Add onions and cook until golden brown.
  5. Add vegetables and sauté for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Add rice, salt and mix gently.
  7. Add water, cover and cook until rice is fully done.
  8. Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot.
