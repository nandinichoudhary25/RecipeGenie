Veg Manchurian

Crispy vegetable balls tossed in a flavorful Indo-Chinese sauce.

⏱ 40 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Medium
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cabbage (grated)
  • ½ cup carrot (grated)
  • ¼ cup capsicum (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili sauce
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Mix cabbage, carrot, capsicum, cornflour and flour.
  2. Form small balls and deep fry until golden brown.
  3. In another pan, sauté ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Add soy sauce, chili sauce and little water.
  5. Add fried balls and toss well in sauce.
  6. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.
