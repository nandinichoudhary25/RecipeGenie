Matar Paneer

A classic North Indian curry made with soft paneer cubes and green peas in a rich tomato gravy.

⏱ 35 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Medium
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 200g paneer (cubed)
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until golden.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates.
  4. Add spices and mix well.
  5. Add peas and cook for 5 minutes.
  6. Add paneer cubes and simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
