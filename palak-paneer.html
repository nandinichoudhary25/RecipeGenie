Palak Paneer

A healthy and flavorful Indian curry made with spinach puree and soft paneer cubes.

⏱ 35 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 250 g Serving)

Calories

350 kcal

Protein

17 g

Carbohydrates

18 g

Fat

25 g

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer (cubed)
  • 2 cups spinach (palak)
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp cream (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil spinach for 2–3 minutes and blend into smooth puree.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Add turmeric and garam masala.
  6. Add spinach puree and simmer for 5 minutes.
  7. Add paneer cubes and cook for 5–7 minutes.
  8. Add cream, mix well and serve hot.
