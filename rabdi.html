Rabdi

A rich and creamy Indian dessert made by slowly reducing milk and flavored with cardamom and nuts.

⏱ 40 mins 🍽 Serves 3 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 200 g Serving)

Calories

320 kcal

Protein

8 g

Carbohydrates

30 g

Fat

18 g

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full cream milk
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder
  • 6–8 chopped almonds
  • 6–8 chopped pistachios
  • A few saffron strands (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat milk in a heavy-bottom pan and bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce flame and simmer, stirring occasionally.
  3. Scrape cream from the sides and mix back into milk.
  4. Cook until milk reduces to half.
  5. Add sugar and cardamom powder.
  6. Cook for 5 more minutes.
  7. Add dry fruits and saffron.
  8. Cool and refrigerate before serving.
