Chocolate Brownie

Rich, fudgy and perfectly gooey homemade chocolate brownies.

⏱ 40 mins 🍽 Serves 6 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup butter (melted)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix melted butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla essence.
  4. Mix flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.
  5. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  6. Pour into greased baking tray.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes.
  8. Cool, cut into squares and serve.
