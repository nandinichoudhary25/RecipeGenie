Samosa

A crispy and delicious Indian snack filled with spiced potatoes and peas.

⏱ 45 mins 🍽 Serves 4 🔥 Medium
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 1 Piece ~120 g)

Calories

250 kcal

Protein

4 g

Carbohydrates

30 g

Fat

12 g

Ingredients

For Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

For Filling:

  • 2 boiled potatoes (mashed)
  • ½ cup green peas
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, oil and salt. Add water and knead stiff dough.
  2. Heat oil and sauté cumin seeds.
  3. Add peas and mashed potatoes.
  4. Add spices and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Roll dough, cut into halves and shape into cones.
  6. Fill with stuffing and seal edges properly.
  7. Deep fry on low flame until golden brown.
  8. Serve hot with chutney.
