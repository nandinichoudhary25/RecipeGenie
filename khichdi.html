Comfort Khichdi

A simple, nutritious and comforting Indian one-pot meal made with rice and lentils.

⏱ 30 mins 🍽 Serves 2 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 250 g Serving)

Calories

320 kcal

Protein

12 g

Carbohydrates

55 g

Fat

8 g

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • ½ cup moong dal
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 cups water

Instructions

  1. Wash rice and moong dal thoroughly.
  2. Heat ghee in a pressure cooker and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add rice, dal, turmeric and salt.
  4. Add water and cook for 3 whistles.
  5. Let pressure release and serve hot with ghee or pickle.
