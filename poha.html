Poha

A light and flavorful Indian breakfast made with flattened rice, peanuts and mild spices.

⏱ 20 mins 🍽 Serves 2 🔥 Easy
← Back to Recipes

Nutrition (Per 200 g Serving)

Calories

250 kcal

Protein

5 g

Carbohydrates

40 g

Fat

8 g

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flattened rice (poha)
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped potato (optional)
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 green chili (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Lemon juice for garnish
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions

  1. Rinse poha lightly in water and drain completely.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds.
  3. Add peanuts and sauté until golden.
  4. Add onions (and potatoes if using) and cook until soft.
  5. Add turmeric and salt, mix well.
  6. Add poha and gently mix everything.
  7. Cook on low flame for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with lemon juice and coriander. Serve hot.
